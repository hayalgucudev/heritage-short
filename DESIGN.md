# Design & Audience Log

## Creative Brief (Locked at Gate 1)
- **Story:** A warm, stylized tribute to `"Grandmother"` in `"the village"` (fictionalized, no real-person likeness).
- **Audience & Platforms:**
  - Family & community on mobile (WhatsApp, TikTok — **9:16** vertical, safe-area English subtitles).
  - Classmates & reviewers on desktop (**16:9** widescreen).
- **Target Ending Feeling:** Quiet gratitude and warmth.
- **Language:** Amharic narration with burned-in English subtitles (2 lines max, ~40 chars/line).
- **Narration Craft & Voice Tone:** `gemini-3.8-flash-tts` (`Aoede`, pure Amharic text with **no English instruction wrapper**). Because pace and warmth cannot rely on an English style prefix, sentences are written short with simple Amharic punctuation (`።`, `፣`). In the Radio Cut review, we will specifically ask test listeners: *"Does the voice feel warm enough?"* and record answers verbatim below.
- **Visual Style Block:** Warm painterly storybook / animated-film look (golden-hour amber light, soft gouache/oil texture, timeless folktale mood, centered framing for dual 9:16 and 16:9 crops).
